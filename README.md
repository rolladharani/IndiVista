# 🌏 IndiVista – Explore India’s Wonders Digitally

## 📌 Project Overview

**IndiVista** is an interactive and visually engaging web application designed to showcase the **cultural and geographical diversity of India**.

It works as a digital travel guide where users can explore India's popular destinations in a structured and user-friendly way. The platform mainly focuses on **Temples, Historical Places, and Waterfalls** across different Indian states.

The website organizes destinations **region-wise** — North, East, West, and South — making it easier for users to explore India based on geographical direction.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of IndiVista is to provide a **clean, organized, and user-friendly web experience** for exploring India's rich heritage and tourism destinations.

The project aims to:

* Provide an easy way to explore Indian states and destinations.
* Organize destinations based on regions and states.
* Showcase popular temples, historical places, and waterfalls.
* Provide a visually appealing and consistent interface.
* Promote digital tourism and awareness of India's cultural and natural heritage.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧭 Region-Wise Exploration

Users can explore Indian states based on four major regions:

* North India
* East India
* West India
* South India

Each region provides access to the states belonging to that geographical direction.

### 🏛️ State-Wise Attractions

Each state page showcases the **Top 10 attractions** in three major categories:

* 🛕 Temples
* 🏰 Historical Places
* 💧 Waterfalls

This allows users to explore destinations in an organized manner.

### 🔥 Trending Top 5

The **Trending Page** highlights the **Top 5 destinations** based on factors such as:

* Climate
* Season
* Best visiting time

This helps users discover popular destinations suitable for different travel periods.

### 🔐 Login Form

The website includes a login interface with fields for user credentials, providing a professional website experience and a foundation for future user personalization.

### 🏠 Home Page

The Home Page acts as the starting point of the website and provides navigation to the major sections of IndiVista.

### 📍 Places Page

The Places Page allows users to select a region and explore its states and destinations.

### 📞 Contact Form

The Contact Page allows users to send inquiries, feedback, or collaboration messages.

### 💬 Review Page

The Review Page provides a space for users to share their experiences and opinions about destinations featured on IndiVista.
